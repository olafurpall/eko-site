  1. Friday 19:42 · incoming call · kitchen and floor staff are mid-service
  2. Sól · AI Ölduhús, gott kvöld. Þú ert að tala við Sól, gervigreindaraðstoðarmann. Hvernig get ég aðstoðað? Ölduhús, good evening. You're speaking with Sól, an AI assistant. How can I help?
  3. Caller Hæ, mig langar að panta borð fyrir sex annað kvöld klukkan sjö. Hi, I'd like to book a table for six tomorrow evening at seven.
  4. Tool call · booking system check_availability(day: "Sat", time: "19:00", guests: 6) 19:00 full · 19:30 window table free
  5. Sól · AI Klukkan sjö er fullbókað, en ég get boðið ykkur borð við gluggann klukkan hálf átta. Hentar það? Seven is fully booked, but I can offer you a window table at half past seven. Does that work?
  6. Caller Já, það hentar fínt. Einn í hópnum er með hnetuofnæmi. Yes, that works. One person in our group has a nut allergy.
  7. Procedure · allergy add_note(booking, "nut allergy") · notify(kitchen) Allergy on the booking · kitchen notified
  8. Sól · AI Ég skrái hnetuofnæmið á bókunina og læt eldhúsið vita. Á hvaða nafni á bókunin að vera? I'll note the nut allergy on the booking and let the kitchen know. What name should the booking be under?
  9. Caller Guðrún.
  10. Tool calls · booking system + SMS create_booking("Guðrún", "Sat 19:30", 6) · send_sms(caller) Booking #2817 confirmed · confirmation text sent
  11. Sól · AI Takk, Guðrún. Borð fyrir sex á morgun klukkan hálf átta. Þú færð staðfestingu í SMS eftir augnablik. Thanks, Guðrún. A table for six tomorrow at half past seven. You'll get a confirmation by text in a moment.
  12. Caller Eitt enn: eruð þið með jólahlaðborð fyrir 40 manna hóp í desember? One more thing: do you do a Christmas buffet for a group of 40 in December?
  13. Sól · AI Viðburðastjórinn okkar sér um jólahópa. Ég gef þér samband við hana núna og sendi henni samantekt, svo þú þurfir ekki að endurtaka neitt. Our events manager looks after Christmas groups. I'll put you through to her now and send her a summary, so you won't need to repeat anything.
  14. Transfer · your team transfer_to_number(events_manager) · summary Guðrún · 40 guests · Christmas buffet · December · already booked Sat 19:30
  15. Result 1 min 22 s with the agent. Table booked, allergy flagged, confirmation sent, and the events manager picks up a caller she already knows about.

Under the hood

Built on ElevenLabs Agents

ElevenLabs Agents handle more than 10 million conversations a week across phone, web, WhatsApp, SMS and email. EKO designs your agent on the platform, connects it to your systems and keeps improving it.

Workflows

Workflows, not one giant prompt

Each call follows a visual workflow. A triage agent routes the caller to specialised subagents, each with its own instructions, tools and knowledge. Handoffs and tool calls are explicit, so the agent behaves the same way every time and can be tested.

Voice

A voice people want to talk to

Expressive Mode pairs Eleven v3 Conversational with smarter turn-taking, so the agent hears tone, reassures a stressed caller and knows when to pause.

Languages

Icelandic, and 70+ more

The agent answers in the caller's language, so Polish, English and German-speaking customers are served too.

ÍslenskaEnglishPolskiDeutsch+66
Procedures

Your procedures, followed

Upload the instructions your staff already use. They become Procedures the agent follows step by step for bookings, refunds or ID checks.

Integrations

Connected to your systems

Booking systems, calendars, CRMs and webshops through APIs and webhooks, plus integrations like HubSpot, Salesforce, Shopify, Zendesk, Stripe and Zapier.

Handoff

A person when it matters

The agent transfers mid-call to a phone number or colleague and passes on a summary, so the caller never repeats themselves.

Testing

Tested before it goes live

Simulated calls, automated tests and guardrails check the agent against your real scenarios before launch and after every change.

Analytics

Every call, measured

Transcripts, success rates, satisfaction scores and languages in one dashboard. EKO uses them to keep improving your agent.

Security

Built for sensitive data

SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified platform with GDPR controls, EU data residency and a zero-retention mode available.

Questions

        
Good to know

      

      

        
Will callers know they're talking to an AI?
Yes. We set the agent up to introduce itself as an AI assistant, and callers can ask for a person at any time.

        
Does it really speak Icelandic?
Yes. ElevenLabs' conversational voice model supports Icelandic among more than 70 languages. You'll hear it on the demo, so you can judge the quality for yourself.

        
What happens when the agent can't help?
It follows the rules you set: it transfers the call to a person, takes a message or books a callback, and always passes on a summary of the conversation.

        
Can we keep our phone number?
Usually, yes. Your existing number forwards to the agent, or it connects through your phone provider.

        
What does it cost?
Two parts: EKO's setup and support, quoted after the demo, and running costs. ElevenLabs charges from $0.08 per call minute, with the phone line and AI model billed on top.

        
Is our customers' data safe?
ElevenLabs is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified, offers EU data residency and a zero-retention mode, and the agent only gets access to the systems it needs.
